Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing, competing, and learning on MathPuzzle.
❓ What is MathPuzzle?

MathPuzzle is a free, interactive mathematical training web application designed to boost mental calculation speed, quantitative problem solving, and analytical intuition. It features real-time multiplayer duels, single-player speed runs, and an entrepreneurial business simulation known as Startup Quest.

💰 Is MathPuzzle completely free to use?

Yes! All game modes, educational guides, categories, and multiplayer features are 100% free to access without subscription fees or paywalls. The platform is supported by non-intrusive banner advertising.

👤 Do I need to create an account or provide an email?

No registration is required. You can jump directly into the game simply by choosing an anonymous GamerId. Your local progress, preferred settings, and high scores are preserved right in your browser.

🤖 How does the AI Adaptive Mode work?

Adaptive Mode continuously monitors your response speed and answer correctness. If you answer consecutive questions quickly and accurately, the system dynamically increases problem complexity. If you hit a roadblock, the engine automatically adjusts to reinforce conceptual building blocks before advancing.

👥 How do I invite friends to a multiplayer duel?

Navigate to the Multiplayer tab on the homepage, choose your category and round duration, and click Create Game. You will receive a unique Room ID and shareable link (e.g., sharphuman.app/join/ROOM_ID) to share with your friends. Up to 8 players can compete in real time.

📱 Can I install MathPuzzle on my phone or tablet?

Yes! MathPuzzle is a certified Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your device without downloading through an app store:

iOS (iPhone/iPad): Tap the Share button in Safari and select Add to Home Screen.

Android: Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome and select Install App or Add to Home screen.

📶 Does MathPuzzle work offline?

Yes! Our Service Worker caches essential game assets and math problem generators. Once loaded or installed as a PWA, Solo Challenge modes remain fully playable offline without an internet connection.

🏫 Can educators or teachers use MathPuzzle in classrooms?

Absolutely! MathPuzzle is widely used in middle school, high school, and university STEM clubs as a fast-paced warmup tool. Teachers can create multiplayer rooms on a projector or smartboard and have students join on their laptops or smartphones.

🔒 How do you protect user data and privacy?

We practice strict data minimization. We do not collect names, emails, passwords, or location data. Read our comprehensive Privacy Policy for complete details.

🐛 How can I report a bug or request a new math category?

We welcome community feedback! Please send error reports, feature suggestions, or mathematical formula inquiries to support@sharphuman.app.

Still have questions?

We are always here to help. Reach out to our team at support@sharphuman.app.
