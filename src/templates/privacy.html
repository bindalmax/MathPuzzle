Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Welcome to MathPuzzle (operated at sharphuman.app). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, educational, and engaging environment. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our website and Progressive Web Application (PWA).

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information like email addresses or real names to play MathPuzzle.

  • Gamer Identifier (GamerId): An anonymous nickname you choose or that is randomly generated (e.g., MathWizard42). This is stored locally on your device and linked to your gameplay scores.
  • Gameplay Data: Problem attempts, accuracy rates, problem solving times, high scores, and adaptive difficulty skill levels to power our AI learning engine and global leaderboard.
  • Technical Information: Standard server logs including IP address, browser type, device information, and timestamps used solely for security, abuse prevention, and rate-limiting purposes.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

MathPuzzle uses cookies and local browser storage (localStorage) to enable essential website functionality:

  • Essential Session Cookies: Secure, HttpOnly session cookies used to preserve game state across turns, handle multiplayer room synchronization, and protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).
  • Local Storage: Storing your preferred GamerId, dark mode preference, and audio settings so you do not have to reconfigure them each time you visit.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements on our website. Please note the following regarding Google advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Children's Privacy

MathPuzzle is designed as a family-friendly educational platform for all ages. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information of a minor has been collected, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

5. GDPR and CCPA / State Privacy Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or California, you are entitled to certain privacy rights:

  • Access & Portability: You may request information about the data we hold relating to your session.
  • Deletion: You may clear your game data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage and cookies, or by clicking "Reset Data" in settings.
  • Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell any personal information to third parties.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:

Email: support@sharphuman.app
Website: https://sharphuman.app
