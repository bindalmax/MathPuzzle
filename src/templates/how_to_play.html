📐 Mental Math Guides & Strategies

📈 Percentages: The 10% & 1% Rule

Never calculate percentages using long multiplication. Break the number into easy power-of-10 chunks:

To find 15% of 240: 10% = 24, 5% = 12 ➔ 24 + 12 = 36

Pro-Tip: Remember that percentages are reversible: x% of y = y% of x. For example, calculating 16% of 50 is identical to calculating 50% of 16 (which is simply 8)!

💰 Profit, Loss & Markup vs Margin

Understanding the difference between markup and profit margin is crucial for business math:

Markup = (Selling Price - Cost) / Cost × 100%
Gross Margin = (Selling Price - Cost) / Selling Price × 100%

If an item costs $80 and you sell it for $100, your markup is 25%, but your gross profit margin is 20%.

📊 Decimals & Fractions: Instant Conversion Table

Memorize these critical fraction-to-decimal benchmarks for split-second answers:

  • 1/8 = 0.125 | 3/8 = 0.375 | 5/8 = 0.625 | 7/8 = 0.875
  • 1/6 ≈ 0.167 | 5/6 ≈ 0.833
  • 1/7 ≈ 0.143 | 2/7 ≈ 0.286

🔢 Algebra: Quick Linear Balance

When solving equations like \(ax + b = c\), mentally combine steps by subtracting \(b\) and immediately dividing by \(a\):

x = (c - b) / a

Example: \(4x + 12 = 36\) ➔ Subtract 12 (gives 24) ➔ Divide by 4 ➔ \(x = 6\).
